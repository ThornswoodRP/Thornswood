# How to join

## What you need

**Skyrim Special Edition** on Steam, on any build. The server runs build
**1.6.1170**, and Thornswood keeps that build in a game folder of its own. If
your Steam copy is on another build, the launcher's **Downgrade** button asks
Steam for 1.6.1170's files under your own account and puts them in
Thornswood's folder. Your Steam copy stays on the build it is on. Nothing
comes from anywhere but Steam.

**Anniversary Edition is not required**, and never will be. If you own it, nothing breaks. If you
do not, nothing is missing.

**Vortex**, with a free Nexus Mods account. The mods come from a collection on
Nexus, and a collection is a Vortex thing: Mod Organizer cannot install it.

Where Thornswood's files go is one box in the install window, **Give
Thornswood a Skyrim folder of its own**, and it starts unticked. Ticked,
Thornswood writes nothing of its own into the Skyrim you already have on Steam,
so that copy stays yours to play and mod however you like: once Vortex has
deployed, the launcher builds a game folder of its own, `Game` in the install
folder on the same drive as Skyrim, out of your Steam copy and what Vortex put
into it, and after that it only takes Vortex's files from there. The mods and
Skyrim's archives are hard links, which take no extra space, and the launcher
checks the archives before every Play; everything else is Thornswood's own
copy. Left unticked, Thornswood's own files go into your Steam copy, with a
list of what went there, and the launcher asks before it builds the folder.
Vortex keeps the mods in a profile of their own, so switching back to your
usual profile and running Purge Mods leaves your own Skyrim the way it was.

## 1. Apply

**Thornswood is eighteen and over.** Applying is you stating that you are. The
[rules](/rules) say what that is about before they say anything else.

**Thornswood is in alpha, and the world is open by application.** Anyone can
download and install it. Play unlocks once you have applied in the Discord and
been given the Playtester role, so applying is the first thing to do.

Read the [rules](/rules) first. Then come into the Discord, open a ticket in
the applications channel and pick **Apply to be an alpha playtester**. It asks
whether you are eighteen or over, whether you accept the rules, and a few lines
about who you want to play and a bug you have run into. It is not an essay and
nobody is marking it. Your answers, the rules one included, are kept against
your Discord account.

Somebody reads every application and answers it. Once you are accepted, your
Discord account is what lets you in. There is nothing else to sign up for, no
second password, and no form on this site.

You can do steps 2 and 3 while you wait. Step 4 works once you are accepted.
It is an alpha, so things break. Say so when you find something: a bug four
people report is a bug that gets fixed.

## 2. Take the installer

One file, from the [Downloads](/downloads) page. Download it and run it.

It is small because the mods are not in it. It fetches Thornswood's own content
while you watch, about half a gigabyte, and a connection that drops carries on
where it stopped rather than starting again. It puts in the launcher, the
script extender and Thornswood's own content, and nothing else.

## 3. Install the collection in Vortex

Not optional. Without it you have a launcher and no mods, and the server turns
you away when you press Play.

The link is on the [Downloads](/downloads) page, on the card above Play and on
the launcher's Diagnostics page. Each opens the revision of the collection the
server was built against and names its number. Install that revision, not a
newer one Nexus may show first: a newer one keeps Play shut until the server
catches up. Vortex takes it from there: it makes a profile for Thornswood,
downloads every mod from its own author, and deploys them. Give it time.

When the server later moves to a newer revision, update the collection inside
Vortex: open Collections and press Update on the Thornswood collection. That
puts the new revision in place of the old one. Installing the new revision
from Nexus instead adds a second Thornswood collection beside the first, and
the two fight over the same files. The launcher's button opens Vortex for this
once a revision is installed.

If the wrong profile is active in Vortex, the launcher says so before you press
Play.

## 4. Connect your Discord in the launcher

Open Thornswood from the desktop shortcut and press **Connect Discord** on the
Play page. Your browser opens, you say yes, and it closes itself.

You never do this again.

## 5. Press Play

Not Skyrim. Not Steam. Not SKSE. Always the Thornswood shortcut.

The launcher checks for updates and takes any that are waiting, because
everybody has to be running the same files or nobody can connect. It also
compares every plugin with the server's copy, byte for byte. If one differs it
says so. For Thornswood's own plugin **Fix** downloads the latest release's
copy; for another author's mod it names the mod to update or reinstall in
Vortex.

Then the game loads to its main menu and lists your characters, with an empty
slot for a new one. Pick one and you are in. There is no address to type and
no password to remember.

## Your first character

A new character arrives in a quiet stone hall in a tunic and boots, and the
race menu opens. That is where you make your face and give your character a
name that belongs in Skyrim.

When you close it, the panel behind **K** opens on its own. It holds the three
choices on the [Professions](/classes) page, your class, your profession and
the half of it you will make your own, and the town you start in: Whiterun,
Riften, Solitude, Windhelm, Markarth, Falkreath, Morthal, Dawnstar or
Winterhold.

Only the town has to be chosen before you leave the hall, and choosing it
sends you there. You arrive at a quiet spot in or near the town, not in its
inn: the stables or the carriage stop at Whiterun, Solitude, Windhelm and
Riften, Understone Keep in Markarth, and a quiet corner of each of the other
four. The town cannot be changed afterwards.

Your class and profession can wait, and are picked later in the world from the
same panel. Until you pick a profession you cannot make anything at a bench,
smelting and tanning included, and until you pick a class every seam, carcass,
plant, fishing spot and crop turns you away. So do not wait long.

You start with the clothes you stand in and forty gold, once. A Miner, a
Farmer, a Fisher and a Woodworker are handed the tool their work needs the
moment they pick it. Everything else you earn, make, buy or are given.

The same panel is behind **K** from then on: your rank, your recipe book and
your skill points.

## Voice

Voice runs in the launcher, not the game, so leave the launcher open while you
play. Hold **V** to talk, or press **O** to keep the microphone open. **B**
steps between a whisper, your normal voice and a yell, which change how far
your voice carries in the world.

## If it goes wrong

Check the [known issues](/known) first: it lists what is broken right now that
the team already knows about. The [help](/help) page has the usual fixes.

If it is not there, say so in the Discord with what you were doing, what you
expected, and what happened instead, or use the [report](/report) page.

The launcher's own log is at `C:\Thornswood\Profile\launcher.log`. Crash logs,
if the game closed itself, are in `C:\Thornswood\Profile\MyGames\SKSE`,
because Thornswood keeps its own My Games folder. In the launcher,
**Diagnostics** then **Build the report** puts them in one zip and opens the
folder it is in: drag that zip into your ticket in the Discord.
