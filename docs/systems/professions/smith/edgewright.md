# Edgewright

Weapons. The [Edgewright recipe list](../../lists/smith-edgewright.md)
gives the counts by rank and shows each recipe's bench, race and whether
it reaches the server.

The wheel is closed. Edgewright or Woodwright, from novice. A
platewright does not temper a blade unless this half is theirs or a
master has taught it. The temper still does not reach the server, so
the closed door and the missing gate are both true: the wrong person
can be turned away, and the right person's temper is not scored.

A recipe has no gold price. What a piece sells for is the price a
person posts.

## Race locks

Read the Race column for a recipe's restriction. Elven and Orcish
weapons are not locked by the name of their metal.

## Handles and charcoal

Swords, greatswords and daggers are a tang. They burn charcoal at a
forge and they do not take a tool handle.

War axes, battleaxes, warhammers, maces, and the other-weapon rows
take one tool handle as well as the charcoal. The handle is novice
charburner work, 1 firewood, 5 gold, and it was not in the ingredients
when the rank was chosen.

The two tools a class pick starts with are novice forge work of their
own, an iron ingot on a handle:

| Item | Rank | Asks for | Makes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pickaxe | Novice | 1 iron ingot, 1 tool handle | 1 |
| Woodcutter's Axe | Novice | 1 iron ingot, 1 tool handle | 1 |

Lockpicks are forge work too. One iron ingot makes 5 of Skyrim's own
lockpicks, the same pick dungeon and body loot hands out, so a pick no
longer comes only from loot (#4646).

## What you are handed

The half places 1 in Precision (+2 critical chance). The game may
ignore that number. The smith profession's Might and Burden still
land. See [Skill tree](../../skill-tree.md).

A Platewright can make novice Edgewright work as their sister half.

Every recipe, by rank and with its id: [smith-edgewright](../../lists/smith-edgewright.md).

## Where this is

The forge gates the craft, including charcoal and the handle.
The sharpening wheel does not count as work. Mastercraft uses a
Weapon Kit instead; see [Durability](../../durability.md).
