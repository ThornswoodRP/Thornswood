# Bladebinder

Staves whose spell is in the College book. The
[Bladebinder recipe list](../../lists/enchanter-bladebinder.md) gives
its counts and shows which spell each staff asks for. The staves left
out of the open book are in [Cut items](../../cut-items.md).

A staff asks for its spell's rank in the book. You unlock a spell only
at a Bladebinder rank at least the spell's. Heart stone is Master
mining, so below that rank you buy it from a Master Miner. See
[Miner](../../classes/miner.md).

## The spell the menu asks for

A staff recipe that names a spell appears only once that spell is on
you. Unlocking it on K puts that spell on you. A spell with the same
name from a different source does not satisfy the recipe.

## Staff spells

You start with 2 points. Each rank before Master gives 1. Master gives
2. The spell's rank cannot pass your Bladebinder rank. A novice spell
needs none below it.

The schools those spells belong to are the mage book:
[Destruction](../mage/destruction.md),
[Restoration](../mage/restoration.md),
[Alteration](../mage/alteration.md),
[Conjuration](../mage/conjuration.md),
[Illusion](../mage/illusion.md).
A staff does not give you the school. It gives you that spell on a
stick, and only if you unlocked it.

## What you are handed

The half places 1 in Potency. With no school held, that point does not
change a spell.

## What you cannot do

You cannot enchant a cuirass at the staff bench. That is Wardweaver,
your sister half at novice. It lets you use the arcane enchanter but
does not hand you Wardweaver's extra Ward point.

## Where this is

The staff enchanter, unlock points and recipe gates are in the server.
The linked list shows the recipe rank and the spell condition.
