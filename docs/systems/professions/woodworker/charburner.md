# Charburner

The kiln is your bench from novice. The book shows its recipes and
which ones reach the server.

The full table: [lists/woodworker-charburner.md](../../lists/woodworker-charburner.md).

| Item | Rank | Asks for | Makes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Poor Charcoal | Novice | 2 firewood | 1 |
| Tool Handle | Novice | 1 firewood | 1 |
| Charcoal | Advanced | 4 firewood | 1 |
| Charcoal Briquette | Expert | 7 firewood | 1 |
| Coke | Master | 12 firewood | 1 |

A forge and a skyforge burn one charcoal per piece. The four grades
are how hot the fire is. A higher metal needs a hotter grade. The
table is on [Smith](../smith.md). A skyforge asks for one grade
poorer, and Poor Charcoal does not get poorer. The charcoal is one of
the piece's ingredients, so the forge's list names the grade and takes
it, and with none of that grade the piece cannot be made, including by
a smith. The Charcoal grade is Skyrim's own Charcoal, so a piece found
in the world burns the same as one from the kiln. Thornswood's older
Charcoal is no longer made and does not fire a forge.

A hafted weapon takes one tool handle on top of its own ingredients,
named in the forge's list with them. The handle was not in those
ingredients when the weapon's rank was set.

## What you are handed

The half places 1 in Toughness (+10 poison and +10 disease
resistance), on top of the woodworker's Endurance and Burden.

## Fire

Master Carver's +48 is this half's kiln as well as a Woodwright's
fire. A kiln or a fire a master woodworker is standing at gives +48
at your feet, to them and to anybody at it with them. Any other kiln
warms like any other fire: +34 at your feet.

## What you cannot do

You cannot make a bow. That is Woodwright, and your sister half is
their novice work only unless a master takes you on.

You cannot chop without the axe. 50 uses, then a smith.

Every recipe, by rank and with its id: [woodworker-charburner](../../lists/woodworker-charburner.md).
