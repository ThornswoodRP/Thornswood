# Hearthcook

Dinners. The [Hearthcook recipe list](../../lists/cook-hearthcook.md)
gives the counts by rank and shows the bench and ingredients for each
portion. A Confectioner using the sister half can make novice meals.

## Festival dishes and brews

Four festival dishes and four festival brews, one of each for each
season, made from that season's two herbs (#3152, [Food](../../food.md),
"Seasonal food"). The loaf, the tart and the cake are the oven, the roast
and the brews the cookpot. A festival dish is a feast and comes out
burnt, plain or fine like any dish; a brew is an ale.

## Benches and the mill

The oven wants Hearthcook or Confectioner from novice. The mill wants
Hearthcook from novice, so a Confectioner does not mill their own
flour unless this half is theirs or a master has taught it. Flour is
what every dessert asks for. A novice confectioner buys it.

The cookpot opens only for somebody who can make a meal there: a
Hearthcook, or a Confectioner for the novice meals. Anybody else is
turned away before the menu opens.

## What you are handed

The half places 1 in Vitality (+10 health), on top of the cook's
Fortitude and Endurance.

One successful meal is one piece of work. 100 pieces to advanced,
then the count starts again at 250, then at 600. Rest per piece is
1.5, 1.2, 0.9, 0.6 by the rank you are in.

## What you cannot do

A master of the trade can teach you more of your sister half.

You cannot fish, hunt, or farm because you cook. The meat and the
vegetables come from those classes. See [Classes](../../classes.md).

Grand Chef's second serving is in the server.

Every recipe, by rank and with its id: [cook-hearthcook](../../lists/cook-hearthcook.md).

## Where this is

The oven, the mill, and the flour recipe are in the
server. The hunger numbers a finished meal grants are partly in the
server (unlisted food is 15, and the food table has its own values)
and the plan's raw, burnt, cooked, and feast steps are on
[Food](../../food.md).
