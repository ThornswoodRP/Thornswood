# Confectioner

Desserts. The [Confectioner recipe list](../../lists/cook-confectioner.md)
gives the counts by rank and shows each recipe's ingredients and bench.
Flour is Hearthcook work at the mill, and wheat is advanced farm work.
A novice Confectioner buys flour.

## What you are handed

The half places 1 in Fortitude. The profession already placed 1 in
Fortitude, so a Confectioner starts at 2: +20% health recovery.

The oven is your bench from novice. The mill is not. The cookpot is
the Hearthcook's, and it opens for you as your sister half, so you
stop at that half's novice list.

## What you cannot do

Read the book for the ranks of the desserts. You cannot skip the
flour or gather eggs through this trade. A chicken nest is the farmer's.

Every recipe, by rank and with its id: [cook-confectioner](../../lists/cook-confectioner.md).
